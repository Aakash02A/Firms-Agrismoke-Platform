Alternatives to burning

Proven ways to stop stubble smoke — and how much cleaner the air becomes with each

Machines that plant without burning

A Happy Seeder or Straw Management System fits onto a normal combine and plants wheat directly into the stubble — no burning needed.

Impact: Every 10% more farms using these machines stops around 180 fires a day and cuts fine particles downwind by about 12 µg/m³.

Bio-spray that turns stubble into manure

Pusa Decomposer, developed by ICAR-IARI, is sprayed on stubble with urea water and breaks it down into natural manure in 20–25 days.

Impact: A low-cost option for small farmers that keeps nutrients in the soil instead of burning them away.

Collecting stubble for other uses

Baled straw can be sold to power plants (as co-firing fuel), bio-CNG plants and packaging makers — turning waste into income.

Impact: Gives farmers money for what they used to burn, removing the reason for open-field fires.

How the numbers are worked out

The same maths and official standards are used for every reading — shown here in full

The Indian National Air Quality Index (AQI) is defined by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change. The sub-index for PM2.5 is determined via piecewise linear interpolation between established regulatory breakpoints:

AQI = [ (I_high - I_low) / (BP_high - BP_low) ] × (C_PM2.5 - BP_low) + I_low

Satellite Fire Detection & FRP Physics

Thermal anomalies are detected in the 3.9 µm and 11 µm mid-infrared channels aboard NASA's S-NPP / NOAA-20 VIIRS instruments (375m spatial resolution). Fire Radiative Power (FRP) in megawatts (MW) measures instantaneous radiant heat energy output, which correlates directly with crop biomass combustion rates ($\sim 0.368 \pm 0.015 \text{ kg/MJ}$).

Where the data comes from

This platform runs on a local Python backend that pulls live satellite fire data from NASA FIRMS and real-time weather and air-quality data from Open-Meteo. When the connection is unavailable, it gracefully falls back to a built-in offline simulation so the dashboard keeps working. Maps, charts and forecasts are computed from that data; no personal information is collected.
